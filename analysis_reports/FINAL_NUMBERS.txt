self-check: 49 tasks | 696 divergences | 522 SIP fires | PS=11 EP=186 RE=42 SA=184 CB=99

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A] FINDING 2 -- the bucket-conditional SIP signature
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The submitted claim is about TASKS, but the evidence offered for it pools FIRES.

  (i)  fire-level chi-square, treating each of the 522 fires as independent:
         G = 50.49, df = 8  ->  p = 3.29e-08
       This test is invalid here: it ignores task clustering, and tasks are
       overdispersed by a factor of four to six.

  (ii) task-clustered permutation over the full count vectors, all 49 tasks:
         G = 50.49  ->  p = 0.1702
       This is the correct test for the claim as stated.

Conclusion: the apparent significance is a unit-of-analysis artefact. The
signature is NOT STATISTICALLY SUPPORTED at the task level.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[B] CEILING BUCKET, paired per-task SA vs EP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one adequately powered paired test available in these data:

  SA > EP on 12 tasks | EP > SA on 12 tasks | 13 ties
  exact two-sided p = 1.0000

  pooled counts SA = 162, EP = 147: the pooled margin is not reproduced at the

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPERSEDED FIGURES AND WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following appear in earlier working files in this repository. They are
superseded; the corrected values are given above.

1. "permutation p = 0.84; the table is indistinguishable from random labelling"
   Superseded. That value comes from an argmax-collapsed test that discards
   roughly 82% of the count information. The correct task-clustered value is
   p = 0.17. The accurate description is "not statistically supported", which
   is weaker than "indistinguishable from random".

2. "the destructive share drops from 54.2% to 35-39%"
   Superseded. That comparison sets fires/fires against divergences/divergences,
   so both terms change. Like for like, the share does not drop.

3. "the 112 structurally-fired EP instances dilute the destructive share"
   Superseded. Applied consistently, removing them RAISES the destructive share
   to 283/410 = 69.0%. The structural-EP decomposition is a construct-validity
   observation about the EP detector and is not a share adjustment.

4. Mid-bucket EP-vs-CB and floor-bucket EP-vs-SA sign tests, offered as
   corroboration of the signature.
   Superseded. With 4 and 1 discordant pairs, the smallest attainable two-sided
   p values are 0.125 and 1.000. These tests cannot reach significance under any
   data and carry no evidential weight. Only the ceiling test in [B] is reported.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE OF THE CORRECTED CLAIM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A power simulation shows that a signature strong enough to support the prose in
the submitted Section 4.3 (delta >= 0.5 to 0.6) would have been detected with at
least 95% probability. The strong form of the claim is therefore excluded by
these data. A moderate signature is not excluded. The accurate statement is that
the signature is not supported; these data do not establish that the effect is
zero.
